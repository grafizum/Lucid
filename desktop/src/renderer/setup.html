One-time permission

Let Lucid read
what you point at.

Lucid needs to see your screen to read the text under the lens. You grant it once, here — then it looks only when you press the lens.

One still frame per press. Not a recording, not a stream — a single snapshot of the lens area.
The icon only reads when you touch it. Idle means idle. Nothing runs in the background.
OCR stays on this machine. Only the extracted text is sent for translation.

Works with any app — WhatsApp, Telegram, a browser, a game. Press Ctrl + Alt + T to read, or right-click the lens for options.
