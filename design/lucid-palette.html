Three warm families and one accent. Cream is the paper the app is printed on, beige is everything raised off it, charcoal is the ink — and the lens, which floats over other people's apps, wears the ink so it always reads. Clay appears three times only: the eyebrow, the live dot, the wash over the words the lens picked.

The mark

cream A · clay 文 · machined plate

The same geometry as today — plus Jakarta A, Noto CJK 文 — recoloured to the system: cream letter, clay second glyph, plate machined from ink_800 to ink_900 with a lit rim. The cream plate is the fallback for light-walled launchers and notification icons.
