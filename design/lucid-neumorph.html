The recipe

One surface, two shadows. Raised means a light from the top-left and a shade to the bottom-right; pressed means the same pair inverted with inset.

raisedpresseddark

Light #FFFCF4 at 95%, shade #C3AE8C at 55–85%, blur 14–22 dp, offset 6–9 dp. On charcoal the light drops to 6% cream and the shade to 55% black.

What it costs

Neumorphism trades contrast for softness: shape alone stops being a reliable affordance, and nothing here passes a 3:1 non-text contrast check against the pad.

So it only works with discipline — every control keeps its word ("Start the lens", "Allowed"), text stays at the ink/sand contrast from the main palette, and the one action you must not miss (the CTA) is the only pressed element on the screen.

Where I would use it

Setup and settings: quiet screens with no time pressure, where the softness reads as calm rather than as missing information. Not on the lens card itself at large — over a stranger's app the card has to be unmistakably ours, and flat charcoal does that better.

This sheet is a study only; nothing in it is built into the app yet.
